`ViewSwitcher` is now a `SegmentedControl` preset (`iconOnly`, `size="sm"`, built-in table, list, grid and board views); its docs and demo are now part of Segmented control. It now looks like a segmented control rather than separate outlined toggle buttons, and its other props are `SegmentedControl`'s rather than `ToggleGroup`'s. Icon-only segments show their label as a tooltip and are at least 44px wide on touch screens.
