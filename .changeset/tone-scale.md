One severity scale, `tone`: `neutral`, `info`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`. `Callout`, `AnnouncementBar` and `NotificationCenter` items rename `variant` to `tone`, `destructive` and `error` become `danger`, and `default` becomes `neutral` (also on `IconText`). `AnnouncementBar` gains `success` and `danger`. `Button` keeps `variant="destructive"`.
